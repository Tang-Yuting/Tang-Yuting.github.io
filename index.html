I am Yuting Tang, a postdoctoral researcher at The University of Tokyo, advised by Takashi Ishida. I am also a visiting scientist at the Imperfect Information Learning Team at RIKEN AIP, led by Masashi Sugiyama. My research interests include reinforcement learning and weakly supervised learning.

Previously, I received my Ph.D. and M.S. from The University of Tokyo, where I was advised by Masashi Sugiyama. Before that, I studied mathematics as an undergraduate student at Dalian University of Technology.
